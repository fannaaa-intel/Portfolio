## Problem

Most period apps assume a regular cycle. They show one "due date" that is often wrong for irregular cycles, and many send sensitive health data to a server or require an account.

## Solution

An offline Android app that logs a period in a few seconds, predicts the next one as a range with an honest confidence level, and keeps all data encrypted on the phone. There are no accounts, no cloud and no analytics.

## What I built

- **Fast logging:** tap a calendar day to set flow, backfill past months by selecting several days at once, and undo any change
- **Honest predictions:** a next-period window with low / medium / high confidence, "still learning" until there are three full cycles, and cycles tagged for illness, travel or stress left out
- **Irregular-cycle care:** late-period handling, cycle phases, fertile-day ranges that switch off when cycles vary too much, and optional ovulation test logging
- **Health notes:** symptoms, daily notes, gentle flags for unusual cycles, symptom patterns and an on-screen summary to show a doctor
- **History:** cycle stats (median, shortest, longest, variation), a cycle-length chart and a cycle list
- **Privacy:** a SQLCipher-encrypted database with its key kept only in secure storage, an optional fingerprint / PIN lock that also blocks screenshots, encrypted backups (AES-256-GCM) and delete-all
- **App:** local reminders with discreet wording, dark mode that passes WCAG AA, and an accessibility pass
- **Under the hood:** prediction and flag logic written as pure, test-first functions, with 64 test files

## Outcome

Shipped as an installable Android APK and in daily use, with over-the-air updates through EAS Update.
