## Problem

Interns on on-the-job training live on a small allowance that goes to fares, food, rent and bills. Tracking that in a notebook or spreadsheet is tedious, so people stop doing it, and nobody knows what their OJT really costs.

## Solution

An offline Android app that makes logging an expense take a second or two, then turns those entries into budgets, alerts and a clear picture of the whole internship's cost. Everything stays on the phone.

## What I built

- **Fast logging:** one-tap meal presets, one-tap daily commute, expense and income forms with undo and full history
- **Commute tools:** saved routes with live fares, an editable fare table, bulk fare updates after a fare hike and route comparison
- **Budgets:** weekly and monthly envelopes with rollover, a "safe to spend today" figure and near / over-budget alerts
- **OJT layer:** hours log, projected end date, cost per day and per hour, and a projected total cost
- **Living costs:** rent and bills, an electricity estimator, roommate split, a grocery list with price memory, savings goals and an emergency fund
- **Data:** reports, an OJT cost PDF, CSV export and JSON backup / restore
- **App:** local reminders, PIN or fingerprint lock, Taglish or English labels, and light / dark / system themes
- **Under the hood:** a local SQLite database (Drizzle ORM, 28 tables), Zustand for state and over 200 unit tests

## Outcome

Shipped as an installable Android APK, with over-the-air updates through EAS Update.
