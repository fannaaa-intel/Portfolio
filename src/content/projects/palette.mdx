## Problem

The client managed sales, stock, suppliers and accounting separately, which caused errors and wasted time.

## Solution

One integrated desktop system on a single shared database. Each staff role (cashier, inventory, procurement, accountant and admin) signs in to its own workspace, so everyone works from the same up-to-date numbers.

## What I built

- **POS:** fast checkout with VAT and Senior / PWD discounts, receipts, returns and refunds, cash handling and transaction history
- **Inventory:** stock levels, low-stock alerts, stock adjustments, put-away and a full movement history
- **Procurement:** purchase requests, supplier quotations, purchase orders, receiving and delivery issues, and supplier payments (including cheques)
- **Accounting:** ledger, income, payables and sales history, with an Income Statement and Balance Sheet by period
- **Admin:** users, roles and permissions, store settings, paint color formulas, an activity log, backups and a recycle bin
- **Security:** login throttling, an idle screen lock and emailed one-time codes for password changes
- **Reports:** printable and exportable to PDF and Excel

## Outcome

Delivered to and used by a local business client.
