## Problem

Local government offices collect citizen feedback but have no easy way to measure or predict how well their services perform.

## Solution

One Flutter codebase delivering both a citizen feedback app and an admin analytics dashboard, on mobile and web.

## What I built

- Mobile and web apps in Flutter from a single codebase
- Supabase backend: PostgreSQL schema, authentication and real-time data sync
- AI-driven analytics that predict and rank public service quality from citizen feedback

## Outcome

Deployed and live for public use at [govpulse.aparri.org.ph](https://govpulse.aparri.org.ph/).
